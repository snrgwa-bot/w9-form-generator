Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Short version. Everything you enter into W-9 Form Generator (the "App") — your name, address, SSN/EIN and signature — stays on your device. We have no user accounts and never collect, store, transmit, sell or share any of it. That data leaves your device only when you choose to export a PDF using iOS share options. The App does send anonymous usage statistics (section 3) that contain none of your form data; you can turn them off in Settings → Privacy → Share Usage Data.

1. Who we are

The App is published by Essential Dev Team ("we", "us", "our"). You can reach us at essentialdevteam@gmail.com.

2. What data the App handles

To prepare a W-9 form, you may enter the following information into the App:

  • Your name and business name
  • Federal tax classification
  • Address (street, city, state, ZIP)
  • Taxpayer Identification Number (SSN or EIN)
  • A drawn signature and the date of signing
  • Optional notes, account numbers and requester information

This information is stored locally on your device using the operating system's secure storage. It is encrypted at rest by iOS using your device passcode and Apple's standard data protection.

3. What we collect: anonymous usage statistics

We never receive your form data. To understand how the App is used and to fix problems, the App sends anonymous usage statistics to our own server (no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs). Each installation gets a random installation ID that is not linked to you, your Apple ID or your device, and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

What is sent:

  • Device model, iOS version and App version
  • Language, region and time zone
  • Accessibility settings that are on (for example larger text or Reduce Motion)
  • When a session starts and ends, and the last screen that was open
  • App events, such as a PDF being generated or shared, a free preview opened, a form duplicated, the app icon changed, or which document types you ask for in "Request a Document" — as categories and counts only (for example "SSN or EIN", "drawn or typed signature", "1099-NEC")
  • Paywall views and which plan was tapped
  • Purchase, trial, renewal, cancellation, refund and restore events (plan type and timing only)
  • When the App asked for an App Store review
  • Crash and performance reports from Apple's MetricKit

What is never sent: your name, business name, address, SSN or EIN, signature, any other text you type, your saved PDFs, your email, phone number or contacts, your location, advertising identifier (IDFA), vendor identifier (IDFV), prices you paid, receipts or transaction IDs. Your IP address is not stored or used.

Retention: usage statistics are kept for 13 months; crash and performance reports for 6 months.

Turning it off: open Settings → Privacy in the App and switch off Share Usage Data. Collection stops immediately and any statistics not yet sent are deleted from your device.

4. What we share

We do not sell, rent, lease or otherwise share any data with third parties. Your form data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself, and the anonymous usage statistics described above are used only by us to improve the App. They are stored with our hosting provider (Supabase), which processes them on our behalf and may not use them for anything else.

To tell you when a new version is available, the App asks Apple's App Store for its latest version number. Only the App's identifier is sent in that request — nothing about you.

5. When data leaves your device

The only time the data you entered leaves your device is when you tap the Export or Share button and choose a destination (email, Files, AirDrop, Print, Save to Photos, etc.). At that point, the destination you chose receives the rendered W-9 PDF. Once it leaves the App, that data is governed by the privacy policy of the receiving app or service (for example Mail, iCloud, or your printer).

6. In-app purchases

The App offers in-app purchases to generate the clean, printable PDF: a one-time "One W-9 Export" purchase and optional auto-renewable monthly and yearly subscriptions. Purchases are processed by Apple via the App Store. We do not see your payment method, billing address, or Apple ID. Apple's privacy policy governs information processed by the App Store: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

7. Contacts permission

If you grant access to your iOS Contacts, the App uses that access only to read the contact you choose and pre-fill name and address fields. The App does not import contact data in bulk, does not upload your contacts anywhere, and never reads SSN, EIN or signature data from contacts. You can revoke contacts access at any time in iOS Settings.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users 17 years of age or older. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age.

9. Your rights

Your form data is never on our side, so you remain in full control of it inside the App. To have the anonymous usage statistics for your installation deleted, copy your Installation ID from Settings → Privacy in the App and email it to us at the address below; we will delete them. You can also stop collection at any time by switching off Share Usage Data. You can delete saved profiles at any time from within the App. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored W-9 data from your device.

10. Security

Your data is protected by the security features of your device, including the device passcode, biometric unlock (Face ID / Touch ID), and iOS Data Protection. We strongly recommend keeping a passcode set on your device.

11. Changes to this policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in the future, we will post the new version at this URL and update the "Last updated" date above. Because we do not collect contact information, we cannot notify users directly — please check this page if you would like to see the latest version.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email essentialdevteam@gmail.com.
